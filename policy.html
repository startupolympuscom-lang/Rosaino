Shipping & delivery

After you place an order, a member of our team calls you to confirm the details. Your order ships once it is confirmed.

  • Major cities are usually delivered within 24 to 48 hours of confirmation; other areas within 48 to 72 hours.
  • Our delivery partner will call before arriving. If you are unavailable, you can reschedule from the order tracking page.
  • You can also update your delivery address from the tracking page until the order has been dispatched.

Cash on delivery

No card is needed online. You pay the courier in cash when your parcel arrives. You are welcome to inspect the package before paying.

The amount due is the total shown at checkout and in your confirmation call. We will never ask you to pay in advance by phone or message.

Returns & exchanges

If something isn't right, you can request an exchange or return within 14 days of delivery.

Eligibility

  • Items must be unused, in their original condition and packaging.
  • For hygiene reasons, opened beauty and care products can only be returned if they are faulty.
  • Items damaged in transit or delivered incorrectly are always eligible. Please contact us within 48 hours with a photo.

How to request a return

Contact us with your order number (for example RS-1024). We will arrange collection and confirm whether you receive an exchange or a refund. Refunds are issued once the item has been received and checked.

Refused deliveries

Repeatedly refusing cash-on-delivery parcels without cancelling in advance may prevent future cash-on-delivery orders.

Privacy

We collect only what we need to deliver your order and support you: your name, phone number, delivery address, the items you order and any notes you add.

  • How we use it: to confirm your order, deliver it, handle returns and answer your questions.
  • Who we share it with: only the delivery partner handling your parcel. We never sell your information.
  • How long we keep it: as long as needed for delivery, after-sales support and our legal obligations.
  • Your rights: you can ask to see, correct or delete your information by contacting us.

Your bag is stored in your browser so it is still there when you return. You can clear it at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Terms of use

By placing an order you confirm that the information you provide is accurate and that you are able to receive and pay for the delivery.

  • Product photos are representative; small variations in colour and finish can occur.
  • Prices and availability may change. The price confirmed during your confirmation call is the price you pay.
  • We may cancel orders that cannot be confirmed, appear fraudulent, or contain a pricing error. You will be told if this happens.
  • All Rosaino names, logos, images and content belong to Rosaino and may not be reused without permission.

Contact

Questions about an order or these policies? Use the contact form or email hello@rosaino.com. Please include your order number if you have one.
